Daily log

Log each night under the date you woke up. On an empty row, drag across the bar to draw the night. Then drag sleep start or wake to adjust it in 15-minute steps. For 5-minute steps, use the arrow keys after dragging. Hours follow the times, so type over them if you slept less than that, or clear the box to go back to the calculated value.

Log a nap on the date it happened. Nap time is added to that date's sleep hours on the charts and in the averages. The Hours box still shows the night alone.
